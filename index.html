A FEW USEFUL DETAILS

Before we
get started.

Can I update my own catalogue or menu?

Yes. Catalogue and menu projects include an admin panel for the content agreed in your proposal. We confirm the available controls and capacity before building.

Are all projects €200?

No. €200 is a starting investment for a limited entry-level scope. Your final quote depends on features, content, integrations and customisation.

Are hosting and a domain included?

No. You pay the domain and hosting providers directly. We agree on a suitable setup before launch. Additional support is quoted separately.

Does a QR menu include online ordering?

The basic menu is for browsing. Ordering, payments and other integrations are optional additions that require a separate quote.

How do we work together?

You communicate directly with Otávio Ribeiro in English by message. Innexa develops projects remotely from Brazil, working with businesses in Galway and beyond.

What do I need to provide?

Your logo, business details, approved text, product or menu information and images you have permission to use. Content creation and translation can be discussed separately.
